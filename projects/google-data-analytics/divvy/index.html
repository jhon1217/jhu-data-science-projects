Recommendations

Turn behavior into growth.

Use the difference in trip purpose to meet casual riders at the moment a membership becomes valuable.

01 · Seasonal offer

Convert during the summer peak

Place short membership trials from May through September, when casual volume is highest and repeat use is most likely.

02 · Place based message

Target leisure hubs

Use station specific prompts at the leading casual origins, pairing ride history with a simple savings estimate.

03 · Weekend product

Bridge recreation to routine

Offer a weekend pass that credits its value toward membership, then follow with weekday commute routes near the rider's usual area.

Process

Ask · Prepare · Process · Analyze · Share · Act

The analysis uses direct aggregate queries against the public trip table, separates members and casual customers, examines seasonality and weekly routines, then connects the strongest patterns to measurable campaign ideas.

Scope

What the data can support

Trip records reveal usage behavior, not motivation. Recommendations should be tested with campaign experiments and customer research. Average duration excludes trips below one minute and above 24 hours to limit operational anomalies.

Source: Divvy Trips, City of Chicago Data Portal. The dashboard covers trips starting between January 1 and December 31, 2019. Aggregates are generated by the reproducible Python project.
